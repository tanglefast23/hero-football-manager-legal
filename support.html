What to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model.
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The game version shown under Settings → Privacy & Support.
  • The screen you were on and the steps that caused the problem.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Career saves

Your career is stored on your device. Hero Football Manager does not require an account and does not keep a developer-operated cloud copy. Deleting the app removes its local game data.

If the game says it cannot save

Keep the game open, free some storage on the device, and use the game’s retry action. Do not delete the app while the save warning is visible.

Response

Support requests are answered as soon as practical. Please do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

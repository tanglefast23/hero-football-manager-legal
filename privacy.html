Privacy Policy

Hero Football Manager is designed to work without an account and to keep your career on your device.

Information the game collects

Hero Football Manager does not collect personal information, gameplay data, or advertising identifiers. The game does not use ads, analytics, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated cloud accounts.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences, player and club names, and career save are stored locally on your device. They are used only to operate the game.

Sharing

The game does not send your gameplay or personal information to Tanglefast or sell it to third parties. Apple may process purchase, download, and optional diagnostic information under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Support email

If you email support, your email provider and our email provider process the address, message, and attachments that you choose to send. We use that information only to answer your request and diagnose the issue you describe.

Deleting your data

You can delete the current career inside the game. Deleting the app removes its local game data from the device. The game does not hold a separate developer account or cloud copy for you to delete.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this page will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to mrjoevu@hotmail.com.
